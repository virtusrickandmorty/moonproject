# A year in the life: the workings (K51)

The expected figures of `apps/server/test/year-in-the-life.test.ts`, worked out from the plan's rules (PLAN D3, D4, D5, D8, F1, F3)
and the BIR tables, independently of the app: nothing here was read from the app or computed by calling its code.
The shop, its people and its documents are made up (AGENTS.md). Amounts are pesos; the test compares centavos.
`tests/golden/year.tb.csv` is the trial balance at the cut-over and at each quarter end that these workings add up to;
`tests/golden/year.returns.csv` has what is typed on each remittance and BIR return, and the totals their worksheets and
the 2316s must show. The dates of every document are in `tools/year-scenario.ts` (the same list, in code).

## 1. Rules used

- **VAT** (D4.1): VAT = round half away from zero of G × 12/112 on the document; NET = G − VAT. 1,120.00 → 120.00; 168,000.00 → 18,000.00;
  84,000.00 → 9,000.00; 33,600.00 → 3,600.00; 44,800.00 → 4,800.00; 11,200.00 → 1,200.00; 8,960.00 → 960.00; 2,240.00 → 240.00; 336,000.00 → 36,000.00;
  80,640.00 → 8,640.00; 100,800.00 → 10,800.00; 5,600.00 → 600.00.
- **Withholding** (D4.5, D4.6, D4.8): on the NET for a VAT-registered payee. EWT: rent 5% of 30,000.00 = 1,500.00; subcontractor 2% of 10,000.00
  = 200.00; goods from the fabric supplier: none, the shop is not a Top Withholding Agent. The academy (a TWA) withholds 1% of the NET of what it
  pays: 1% × 75,000.00 = 750.00 on each half of 168,000.00 (1% × 70,000.00 = 700.00 on the 78,400.00 balance after the credit memo; 1% × 40,000.00
  = 400.00 on the opened 44,800.00). The municipal office withholds 5% VAT (15,000.00) and 1% CWT (3,000.00) of the NET 300,000.00, 2307 in hand.
- **Deposit VAT modes** (D3): A deposit only; B VAT on the deposit (Dr 2209 / Cr 2301 at the downpayment, back at the invoice); C an invoice on
  the downpayment (NET to 2201, moved to sales by the release invoice for the balance).
- **Payroll** (F1, F3): contribution month = month of the period end; SSS, PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG are the month's amount on month-to-date pay less
  what earlier runs of the month took. SSS: MSC = pay to the nearest ₱500 (₱250 up), ₱5,000–₱35,000, EE 5%, ER 10%, EC ₱10 up to MSC ₱14,500 else
  ₱30. PhilHealth 5% of the monthly basis, split equally (each half rounded): monthly staff the monthly rate; daily-paid the daily rate × 313/12
  (550 → 14,345.83; 600 → 15,650.00) plus, for daily-and-piece pay, the month's piece pay; a piece worker the month's piece pay but at least the
  minimum wage's 14,345.83. Pag-IBIG 2% each on pay up to ₱10,000. Semi-monthly withholding tax (BIR RR 11-2018 Annex E, 2023 on) on pay less the
  employee shares: 0 to 10,417; 15% over 10,417; 937.50 + 20% over 16,667; 4,270.70 + 25% over 33,333. A minimum wage earner (the sewer) is not taxed on
  the minimum wage. 13th-month accrual = basic pay of the run ÷ 12 (overtime, SIL cash and allowances are not basic). Government loans are deducted
  on the run ending on or after the 16th; the cash advance instalment on every run until paid.
- **Year-end adjustment** (RR 11-2018, the 16–31 December run of each year): annual table 0 to 250,000; 15% over 250,000; 22,500 + 20% over 400,000;
  102,500 + 25% over 800,000, on the year's taxable pay (with the pay before Moonproject for 2026) less shares, plus 13th month and benefits above
  ₱90,000; less the tax withheld before in the year.
- **SIL** (F1): 5 days a year after a year of service; days not taken are paid in cash on the December run ("Pay unused leave"): monthly staff at
  the equivalent daily rate (monthly × 12 ÷ 313: 40,000 → 1,533.55; 16,000 → 613.42), daily-paid at the daily rate, the piece worker at the average
  daily piece pay of the last 7 workdays, not below the minimum wage (550.00: the last 7 workdays of December hold no piece work). The first 10 days a
  year paid in cash are de minimis: not taxable. They are compensation for SSS (so December's SSS is on pay + SIL) but not PhilHealth's basic salary.
- **13th month** (PD 851, TH13-PAY): paid on 18 December 2026 and 17 December 2027 for both groups: 1/12 of the basic pay of the payrolls recorded
  to that day and not yet counted, against what the runs accrued on 2111 (the difference to 6103 / 5204). So the 16–31 December payroll of 2026 is
  counted in the 13th month of 2027, and the one of 2027 stays accrued on 2111 at the end.
- **Income tax** (1702Q, 1702-RT): whole pesos; regular 20% of taxable income (a small corporation), MCIT 2% of gross income (operations since 2019,
  so it applies), the higher; itemized deductions. The 2307s of each quarter are in hand at its end.
- **Due dates** (TAX calendar, research §3.8): the 10th of the next month for the 1601-C (December: 15 January) and the 0619-E (months 1 and 2 of a
  quarter); the 25th after the quarter for the 2550Q; the last day of the next month for the 1601-EQ; 60 days after Q1–Q3 for the 1702Q; 15 April
  for the 1702-RT; a Saturday, Sunday or holiday on file moves it to the next working day. SSS, PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG are remitted with the 1601-C.
