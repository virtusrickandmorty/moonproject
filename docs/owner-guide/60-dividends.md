# 60. Dividends

**What it is for:** Declare a cash dividend and pay the money to the stockholders.

**Before you start:** You will need the board resolution for the dividend. You must know the record date. You also need to know the amount per share, or the total amount for the dividend.

### Steps
To declare a dividend:
1. On the **Money** menu, click **Dividend Declarations**.
2. Click **New Dividend Declaration**.
3. Type the **Resolution number**, the **Resolution date**, and the **Record date**.
4. Pick the **Basis** and type the **Amount**.
5. Type a **Note** if you want.
6. Click **Record**.

To pay a stockholder:
1. On the **Money** menu, click **Dividend Payments**.
2. Click **New dividend payment**.
3. Under **Which stockholder was paid?**, pick the stockholder from the list.
4. Under **Where did the money come from?**, pick the cash place.
5. Type the **Amount paid (the dividend less the final tax withheld)**.
6. Type a **Note** if you want.
7. Click **Record**.

### What the system does for you
When you declare a dividend, the system splits the amount by the shares held at the end of the record date. For individual stockholders, it withholds a 10% final tax from their dividend. The system records the net amount as payable to the stockholder. The withheld tax shows on the **1601-FQ** return for that quarter. When you record a payment, the system takes the money from the cash place and lowers the payable balance for that stockholder.

### Common mistakes and how to fix them
- **Mistake:** The system stops you from declaring a dividend because of retained earnings.
- **Fix:** You cannot declare more than the retained earnings available on the declaration date. You must cancel and redo with a smaller amount, or wait until the business makes more money.
- **Mistake:** A stockholder is missing their TIN.
- **Fix:** The system will show a warning because the 1601-FQ and 1604-F returns need the TIN. Go to the **Owners and officers** screen and add the TIN before filing the tax return.
