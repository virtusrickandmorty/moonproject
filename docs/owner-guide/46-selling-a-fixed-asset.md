# 46. Selling a fixed asset

**What it is for:** Record taking a fixed asset off the books when you sell it to a buyer or retire it. For **Retired**, nothing is received for it: the book value left is charged as a loss.

**Before you start:** Run the month's depreciation first, so the book value is up to date. You will need to know if it is being sold or retired. If sold, it must be paid in full now; a bank transfer counts. You need the invoice from the booklet, what the buyer paid (VAT included), where the money went, and the buyer's details.

### Steps
1. On the **Money** menu, click **Fixed assets**.
2. Find the asset in the list and click on its number.
3. Click the **Dispose of this asset** button to open the "Take <asset> off the books" dialog.
4. Choose **Sold** or **Retired**.
5. Type **Why is it being taken off?**.
6. If sold, type the **Invoice number (from the booklet)** and the **Price, VAT included**. Pick **Where did the buyer's money go?** and the **Buyer** (or type the **Buyer's name**, **Buyer's TIN** and **Buyer's address**).
7. If **Sold**, check the figures shown in the panels before saving. If **Retired**, nothing is shown but the loss in the summary. Click **Record the disposal**.

### What the system does for you
For a sale, before saving, it calculates and shows the **Book value and gain or loss** to tell you the **Gain on the sale** or **Loss on the sale** (if you sold it for more or less than its book value). It also shows **Write these on the booklet** with the **VATable sales**, **VAT**, and **Total**, so you can write these on the booklet.

### Common mistakes and how to fix them
- **Mistake:** You entered the wrong price or picked the wrong buyer.
- **Fix:** You cannot change a disposal once recorded. Go to the asset and click the disposal document number. Click **Cancel**, give a reason, and click **Cancel document**. The document stays on file, marked Cancelled, and everything it recorded is reversed with today's date. Then, redo the disposal with the correct details.
