# 43. Home, Search and TV Board

**What it is for:** See your daily alerts on the home screen, find any record quickly using search, and display the production status on a large screen in the workroom.

**Before you start:** You will need to sign in to the system. Each person sees only what their role allows them to see.

### Steps
1. **Home screen:** On the **Overview** menu, click **Home**.
2. **Search:** Click or tap the **Search customers, JOs, numbers…** box at the top of the screen and type what you are looking for.
3. **TV board:** On the **Production** menu, click **TV board**.

### What the system does for you
- **On the Home screen:** It shows a **Notifications** panel with alerts that need your attention. You can click **Mark read** when you are done with an alert, or click **See all notifications** to view older ones. It also shows summary widgets for your role, where you can click **Open board** to see more details.
- **When searching:** As you type in the search box, the system instantly suggests matches for customer names, job order numbers, and document numbers. Click on a suggestion to go straight to that record.
- **On the TV board:** It displays a large **Production** board showing all active jobs. The screen automatically updates, saying **Refreshes every 30 seconds**, so you never need to reload it. Urgent orders stand out with a red **RUSH** indicator.

### Common mistakes and how to fix them
- **Mistake:** You cannot find a document or see a specific notification on your home screen.
- **Fix:** You might not have permission for that part of the system. The owner must go to Users or Roles and permissions to give you access.
