# 27. Credits and Write-offs

**What it is for:** Handle special changes to what a customer owes. You can record a 2307 when no cash came in, forfeit a deposit, record a credit memo, or write off a bad debt.

**Before you start:** Know which customer and invoice or job order you are changing.

### Steps

#### Recording a 2307 with no cash
Use this for a customer's 2307 when no cash came in.
1. On the **Sales** menu, click **2307s Received**.
2. Click **+ New**.
3. Pick the invoice.
4. Type the **Tax withheld (on the 2307)**.
5. Pick the **ATC** and the **Period**.
6. Click **Record**.

#### Forfeiting an abandoned deposit
The owner or accountant may forfeit a deposit when a customer abandons a job order.
1. On the **Sales** menu, click **Deposit Forfeits**.
2. Click **+ New**.
3. Pick the **Customer** and **Which job order?**.
4. Type the **Amount kept** or click **All of it**.
5. Type **Why is it kept? (at least 10 characters)**.
6. Click **Forfeit**.

#### Recording a credit memo for a return or allowance
The owner or accountant may post a credit memo for a return or an allowance.
1. On the **Sales** menu, click **Credit Memos**.
2. Click **+ New**.
3. Pick the invoice.
4. Pick **Return (goods came back)** or **Allowance (price reduced)**.
5. Type the **Amount, with VAT** or click **All that is left**.
6. Type the manual **Credit memo form no. (if one is used)**.
7. Type **Why? (at least 10 characters)**.
8. Click **Record**.

#### Writing off a bad debt
The accountant may write off a bad debt.
1. On the **Sales** menu, click **Bad Debt Write-offs**.
2. Click **+ New**.
3. Pick the invoice.
4. Type **Why is it written off? (at least 10 characters)**.
5. Click **Write off**.

### What the system does for you
- **2307s Received:** It takes the tax off what the invoice owes.
- **Deposit Forfeits:** It marks the job order as abandoned. This means nothing more is released or invoiced on it. It keeps the money as other income.
- **Credit Memos:** It makes your output VAT go down. The amount is taken off what the invoice owes. Any part that was already paid is kept as customer credit.
- **Bad Debt Write-offs:** It writes off the bad debt, but the output VAT stays. Collections on it are refused until the write-off is cancelled.

### Common mistakes and how to fix them
- **Mistake:** You recorded a form with the wrong amount or the wrong invoice.
- **Fix:** Never delete it. View the document and click **Cancel** (cancelling each one reverses its effects). You can then record a new one to fix the mistake.
