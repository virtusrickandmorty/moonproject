# 55. Customer checks

**What it is for:** Keep track of the checks customers give you. See which checks are waiting for their date, which are ready to deposit, and handle checks that the bank returns.

**Before you start:** You need the check number, the bank, and the check date to record a check.

### Steps

**Taking a check on a collection:**
1. When recording a collection, click the checks account as the place the money went to.
2. Fill in **Check no.**, **Bank** and the date on the check.

**Adding a post-dated check:**
1. On the **Sales** menu, click **Post-dated checks**.
2. Click **+ Add a post-dated check**.
3. Pick the customer, then fill in **Check number**, **Bank**, **Date on the check** and **Amount**. If the customer has job orders, tick the ones the check is **For**. You can type a **Note**. Click **Add to the list**.
4. When the check is due, its status says **Due: record it now**. Click **Record the collection** next to it. If the customer gave a new check instead, click **Void** and type a reason.

**Depositing a check:**
1. On the **Money** menu, click **Checks on hand**.
2. Tick the checks you want to deposit.
3. Choose the **Bank** to deposit them into.
4. Click **Deposit the ticked checks**. Read the box **Record this deposit?**, then click **Record**.

**A check the bank returned:**
1. On the **Money** menu, click **Checks on hand**.
2. Find the check under **Deposited checks (if the bank returns one)**.
3. Click **Returned by the bank**.
4. Type the reason the bank gave. Fill in the **Bank charge for the returned check** (leave it blank if none). The box that cancels the collection is ticked; untick it only if the customer asked you to deposit the check again. Click **Record the return**.

### What the system does for you
A post-dated check on the **Post-dated checks** list is only a reminder: nothing is in the books until you record the collection. A check recorded on a collection goes to **Checks on hand**, where it stays until you deposit it. Depositing records one transfer from Checks on hand to the bank. If the bank returns a check, the check comes back to Checks on hand, the bank charge is recorded, and the collection is cancelled (unless you unticked it), so the customer owes the money again.

### Common mistakes and how to fix them
- **Mistake:** You want to cancel a collection, but its check was already deposited.
- **Fix:** The app says the check was deposited. If the bank returned it, record that on **Checks on hand** first. If the deposit was a mistake, cancel the deposit first, then the collection.
- **Mistake:** You see a message that the books hold more or less in Checks on hand than the list.
- **Fix:** Ask your accountant to look at the cash book.
