# 55. Customer checks

**What it is for:** Keep track of the checks customers give you. See which checks are waiting for their date, which are ready to deposit, and handle checks that the bank returns.

**Before you start:** You need the check number, the bank, and the check date to record a check.

### Steps

**Taking a check on a collection:**
1. When recording a collection, pick a checks account for the payment.
2. Fill in the **Check number**, the **Bank**, and the **Date on the check**.

**Adding a post-dated check:**
1. On the **Sales** menu, click **Post-dated checks**.
2. Click **+ Add a post-dated check**.
3. Fill in the check details and click **Add to the list**.
4. When the check becomes due, click **Record the collection** next to it on the list.

**Depositing a check:**
1. On the **Money** menu, click **Checks on hand**.
2. Tick the checks you want to deposit.
3. Choose the **Bank** to deposit them into.
4. Click **Deposit the ticked checks**.

**A check the bank returned:**
1. On the **Money** menu, click **Checks on hand**.
2. Find the check under the Deposited checks (if the bank returns one) list.
3. Click **Returned by the bank**.
4. Fill in the **Bank charge for the returned check**, then click **Record the return**.

### What the system does for you
The system keeps a list of your **Post-dated checks** separately until their date arrives. When a check is due or recorded directly on a collection, it goes to **Checks on hand**. The system groups these checks so you can easily deposit them together. If a check bounces, the system can cancel the collection so the customer owes the money again, and it logs the bank charge.

### Common mistakes and how to fix them
- **Mistake:** You want to cancel a check's collection, but it is already deposited.
- **Fix:** A check's steps are undone only in the order they happened, newest first. You cannot undo a collection if the check is deposited. You must undo the deposit first. If the bank returned it, you must undo the return first. Always go backwards step by step.
