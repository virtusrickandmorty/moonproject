# 59. Reversing journal vouchers

**What it is for:** Mark a journal voucher to reverse on the first day of next month, and record the reversal when it is due. You use this for accruals.

**Before you start:** Know the accrual you are recording, or which marked voucher is due to be reversed.

### Steps
**To mark a voucher to reverse:**
1. On the **Accounting & Tax** menu, click **Journal Vouchers**, then click **+ New Journal Voucher**.
2. Fill in the voucher as usual, and tick **Reverse on the first day of next month (an accrual). It shows in Reversals due from that day.**
3. Click **Record**, read the box **Record this Journal Voucher?**, then click **Record** again.

**To record the reversal:**
1. On or after the first day of the next month, on the **Accounting & Tax** menu, click **Reversals due**.
2. Find the voucher in the list and click **Reverse**. A new journal voucher opens with the same lines, debits and credits swapped. A note reads "This reverses [number] ... Record it as it is."
3. Click **Record**, read the box, then click **Record** again.

### What the system does for you
It lists the marked vouchers in **Reversals due** from the first day of the next month until they are reversed. **Reverse** fills in the reversal for you, dated the first day of that month even if you record it a few days later. A voucher is reversed only once.

### Common mistakes and how to fix them
- **Mistake:** You expect the voucher to reverse by itself on the first day of the next month.
- **Fix:** Nothing reverses by itself. Go to **Reversals due**, click **Reverse**, and record the new voucher.
- **Mistake:** You see "No reversal is due."
- **Fix:** No marked voucher has reached its day yet, or it was already reversed.
- **Mistake:** You recorded a reversal wrongly.
- **Fix:** You cannot delete it. Open it and click **Cancel**, type a reason, and click **Cancel document**. It then shows again in **Reversals due**.
