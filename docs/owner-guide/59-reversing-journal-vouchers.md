# 59. Reversing journal vouchers

**What it is for:** Mark a journal voucher to reverse in the future, and record the reversal when it is due. You use this for accruals.

**Before you start:** You will need to know which journal voucher to mark, or if one is already due to be reversed.

### Steps
1. When recording a new journal voucher, check **Reverse on the first day of next month (an accrual). It shows in Reversals due from that day.**
2. On the first day of the next month, go to the **Accounting & Tax** menu and click **Reversals due**.
3. Find the voucher in the list and click **Reverse**. This opens a new journal voucher with the same lines, but debits and credits swapped, dated that day.
4. Review the voucher and click **Record**.

### What the system does for you
The system keeps track of vouchers marked for reversal. When the first day of the next month arrives, it shows them in the **Reversals due** list. Clicking **Reverse** prepares the reversal voucher for you so you don't have to type the lines again.

### Common mistakes and how to fix them
- **Mistake:** You expect the voucher to reverse automatically on the first day of the next month.
- **Fix:** Nothing reverses by itself in Moonproject. You must go to the **Reversals due** screen and click **Reverse**, then record the new voucher yourself. If you made a mistake, you can cancel the recorded reversal and redo it.
