# 7. Cash Accounts and Count

**What it is for:** Set up the cash boxes, bank accounts, and e-wallets you use, then count the actual bills and coins in a cash box to record the total.

**Before you start:** Know exactly what kind of account you are adding (cash box, bank, e-wallet) and the physical money you have.

### Steps
1. On the **Money** menu, click **Cash Accounts**.
2. Click **Add place**.
3. Type the **Name** and choose the **Kind** (Cash box, Checks on hand, Bank, or E-wallet).
4. For banks and e-wallets, you can type an **Account number**.
5. Click **Save**.
6. To count the money in a cash box, go to the **Money** menu and click **Cash Counts**.
7. Click **+ New cash count**.
8. Under **Cash box**, pick the one you counted.
9. Under **Bills and coins**, type the **Quantity** of each **Denomination** you have.
10. Add a **Note** if needed.
11. Click **Record** (or **Save draft** if you want to finish later).

### What the system does for you
It safely stores your account details and calculates the current cash balance. When you record a count, it keeps a history of exactly how many of each bill or coin you had at that time, and calculates the total amount for you.

### Common mistakes and how to fix them
- **Mistake:** You entered the wrong quantity for a bill or coin.
- **Fix:** You cannot delete a recorded count. To fix a mistake, just cancel and redo it. You can open the count and choose to edit it, which will ask for a reason, cancel the old count, and let you record a new one with the correct quantities.
