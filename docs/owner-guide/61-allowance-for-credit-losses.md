# 61. Allowance for credit losses

**What it is for:** Choose how to handle bad debts, record an allowance for amounts that might not be collected, and write off uncollectible invoices against that allowance.

**Before you start:** You will need to check your shop's settings to see if bad debts are written off directly or through an allowance. You will also need to know the customer's name and the invoice to write off.

### Steps

**To set the bad debt method:**
1. On the **Accounting & Tax** menu, click **Settings**.
2. Look for the **Bad debts** setting.
3. Choose either **Written off directly to bad debts (6270)** or **Allowance for credit losses (1209), written off against it**.

**To record the allowance:**
1. On the **Credits & Write-offs** menu, click **Allowance for Credit Losses**.
2. Choose if the allowance is calculated **in total** or **per customer**.
3. Type the suggested amount you want to hold as the allowance.
4. Click **Record**.

**To write off an invoice:**
1. On the **Credits & Write-offs** menu, click **Bad Debt Write-off**.
2. Pick the customer and the invoice.
3. Fill in **Why is it written off? (at least 10 characters)**.
4. Click **Write off**.

### What the system does for you
The system keeps track of the total allowance you have set. When you record a **Bad Debt Write-off**, it deducts the invoice amount from the allowance instead of directly charging the bad debts expense account.
You can see the allowance on the **AR aging** report (under the **Reports** menu) where it shows as **Less allowance for credit losses** deducted from total receivables to give the **Net receivables**. The allowance also appears as a deduction from accounts receivable on the **Balance sheet** (under the **Reports** menu).

### Common mistakes and how to fix them
- **Mistake:** You try to write off an invoice under the allowance method, but the allowance is too low.
- **Fix:** Cancel the write-off, record a new **Allowance for Credit Losses** to increase the allowance, and then redo the write-off.
