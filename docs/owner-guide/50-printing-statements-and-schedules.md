# 50. Printing statements and schedules

**What it is for:** Print important documents like a customer's statement of account, a wearer's sizing profile, or the fixed asset schedule.

**Before you start:** Make sure you have a working printer connected to your computer.

### Steps

**To print a customer's statement of account:**
1. On the **Reports** menu, click **Customer statement**.
2. Pick the customer in the **Customer** field.
3. Type the **From** and **To** dates.
4. Click **Show**.
5. Click **Print statement of account**.

**To print a wearer's sizing profile:**
1. On the **Customers** menu, open a customer.
2. In the **Wearers** panel, click on a wearer's name to open their **Measurements**.
3. Click **Print sizing profile**.

**To print the fixed asset schedule:**
1. On the **Reports** menu, click **Fixed-asset schedule**.
2. Type the **As of** date.
3. Click **Show**.
4. Click **Print fixed asset schedule**.

### What the system does for you
The system builds a document ready for printing and opens it in a new window. It gathers all the lines, balances, and totals for statements and schedules. It formats sizing profiles so they are easy to read. Note that the customer's statement of account will say it is not valid for claiming input tax. This is because only official sales invoices or official receipts can be used for taxes, not statements.

### Common mistakes and how to fix them
- **Mistake:** You click the print button but nothing happens, and you see a message saying "Allow pop-ups for this site, then try Print again."
- **Fix:** Your web browser is blocking the print window. Look at the top right of your browser address bar for a blocked pop-up icon. Click it, choose to always allow pop-ups for this site, and try again.
