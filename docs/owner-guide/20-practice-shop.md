# 20. The Practice Shop

**What it is for:** Train with made-up data. Nothing you record, print or change reaches the real shop or its books.

**Before you start:** You must be on a joined phone or PC.

### Steps
1. On the **Admin** menu, click **Practice shop**.
2. Click the address link to open it on a joined phone or PC.
3. Sign in with your usual username and password.
4. You will see a yellow band showing you are in practice. Printouts will also say they are not real documents. Backups are not part of it.
5. To start over (owner only), go back to the real shop and open the **Practice shop** page.
6. Click **Start the practice shop over** and type your password. This throws away everything recorded in practice.

### What the system does for you
It runs a made-up shop right next to the real one. It keeps them totally apart, so you can train safely.

### Common mistakes and how to fix them
- **Mistake:** You do not know if you are working in the real shop or the practice shop.
- **Fix:** Always look for the yellow band at the top. If you see "PRACTICE SHOP", you are safe to practise.
