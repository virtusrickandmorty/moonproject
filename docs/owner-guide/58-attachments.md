# 58. Attachments

**What it is for:** Attach a photo or a PDF to a document. This is useful for keeping a record of a signed form, a receipt, or a picture of a job order.

**Before you start:** You will need the file saved on your PC, or have your phone ready to take a picture. You must have a document already recorded on the screen.

### Steps
1. Scroll down to the **Attachments** section at the bottom of the document.
2. Click **Add a file** to choose a file from your PC. If you are on a phone, you can also tap **Take a photo** to use your camera right away.
3. To view an attachment later, click its file name in the list.
4. To remove an attachment, click **Remove** next to its name.
5. In the box that asks **Remove [filename]?**, type a reason and click **Remove**.

### What the system does for you
It checks that your file is allowed. You can only attach a **JPEG, PNG, WebP or PDF, up to 10 MB each**. It keeps track of how many files you have. You can have up to **10 attached** per document.
When you remove an attachment, it stays on file, listed as removed with your reason, and can still be opened. Nothing the document recorded changes.

### Common mistakes and how to fix them
- **Mistake:** You attached the wrong picture by mistake.
- **Fix:** Click **Remove** next to it. Type a reason like "wrong file" and click **Remove**. Then, click **Add a file** to upload the correct one.
- **Mistake:** You cannot attach a file because it says it is too big.
- **Fix:** The file must be under 10 MB. If it is a photo, try taking a smaller picture. If it is a PDF, ask the sender for a smaller copy.
