# 58. Attachments

**What it is for:** Attach a photo or a PDF to a recorded document, like a signed form, a receipt, or a picture of a job order.

**Before you start:** Have the file saved on your PC, or your phone ready to take a picture. Open the recorded document first.

### Steps
1. On the document, find the **Attachments** section (below **What this did**).
2. Click **Add a file** to choose one or more files from your PC. On a phone, you can also tap **Take a photo** to use the camera.
3. To open an attachment, click its file name in the list.
4. To remove an attachment, click **Remove** next to it.
5. In the box **Remove [file name]?**, type a **Reason (at least 10 characters)** and click **Remove**. Click **Go back** if you change your mind.

### What the system does for you
It takes only a **JPEG, PNG, WebP or PDF, up to 10 MB each**, and up to 10 attached files per document. The line under the buttons shows how many are attached, like "3 of 10 attached". Each file shows who added it and when. A removed file stays on file, crossed out, with who removed it and the reason, and can still be opened. Nothing the document recorded changes.

### Common mistakes and how to fix them
- **Mistake:** You attached the wrong file.
- **Fix:** Click **Remove** next to it, type a reason like "wrong file attached", and click **Remove**. Then click **Add a file** to attach the right one.
- **Mistake:** You see "[file name] is bigger than 10 MB. Attach a smaller picture or PDF."
- **Fix:** Take a smaller picture, or ask the sender for a smaller PDF.
- **Mistake:** You do not see **Add a file** or **Remove**.
- **Fix:** Only users who may make that kind of document can attach or remove files. Ask the owner.
