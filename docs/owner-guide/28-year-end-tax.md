# 28. Year-end tax and 2316

**What it is for:** Add pay before Moonproject or from a previous employer, do the year-end tax adjustment on the last December payroll, and view the 2316 and alphalist.

**Before you start:** For each employee who had pay before Moonproject or from a previous employer this year, go to their page. Scroll down to the **Pay before Moonproject and from previous employers** section and record the figures. You get the previous employer's pay from its 2316.

### Steps
1. When recording the last payroll of the year for a pay group, tick **Year-end tax adjustment**.
2. The preview will show each employee's tax adjustment for the whole year. A refund will show under **Tax refund**, and a shortage will show as a **Deficiency**. Record the payroll as usual.
3. To view or print the forms for a year, go to the **People & Payroll** menu and click **2316 and alphalist**.
4. Pick the year. You can click **2316** next to an employee to see or print their form.
5. To download the files for the BIR data entry, click **1604-C schedule 1 (CSV)** and **1604-C schedule 2, minimum wage earners (CSV)**.

### What the system does for you
It calculates the exact tax due for the whole year, adding up the Moonproject payrolls, the 13th-month pay, and any pay before Moonproject and from previous employers. It then compares this to the tax withheld. If too much was withheld, it gives a **Tax refund** that is paid out with the net pay. If too little was withheld, it takes the **Deficiency** from the pay. It also works out if an employee qualifies for **Substituted filing**.

### Common mistakes and how to fix them
- **Mistake:** You realized an employee had a 13th-month pay or another payroll recorded after the year-end adjustment was done.
- **Fix:** The later pay changes the year's totals, so the adjustment is no longer correct. You need to cancel the payroll that has the year-end adjustment and do it again.
