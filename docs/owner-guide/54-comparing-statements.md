# 54. Comparing Statements

**What it is for:** View the income statement or balance sheet for a certain period alongside the previous month or the same period last year to see how the business is doing over time.

**Before you start:** Know whether you want to look at the income statement or the balance sheet, the dates you are interested in, and what period you want to compare it to.

### Steps
1. On the **Reports** menu, click **Income statement** or **Balance sheet**.
2. If you chose the income statement, type the **From** and **To** dates. If you chose the balance sheet, type the **As of** date.
3. Under **Compare with**, pick **Previous month** or **Same period last year**.
4. Click **Show**.

### What the system does for you
It shows the statement with two columns of amounts side-by-side. The first column is the period you asked for, and the second column is the period you chose to compare it to.

It also adds two more columns:
- **Difference:** How much the amount went up or down compared to the earlier period.
- **Difference %:** The difference shown as a percentage of the earlier amount.

If an account did not have any money in it during one of the periods, that space will just be blank instead of saying zero. Also, if the amount in the earlier period was zero, the **Difference %** will be blank. This is because you cannot calculate a percentage change when starting from nothing.

You can click **Export CSV** to download a copy of the numbers to open in Excel, or click **Print** to get a paper copy.

### Common mistakes and how to fix them
- **Mistake:** You chose a comparison period but clicked Show without typing the dates first.
- **Fix:** Make sure you fill in the **From** and **To** dates (for the income statement) or the **As of** date (for the balance sheet) before clicking **Show**.
